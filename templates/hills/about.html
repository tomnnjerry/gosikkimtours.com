About us

The Eastern Himalaya, nothing else

Go Sikkim Tours plans affordable trips in Sikkim, the Darjeeling hills, Kalimpong, the Dooars and Bhutan. We work only here because we know the roads, the permit offices, the jeep stands and the homestays well enough to plan them honestly.

Most people who visit the hills are families, couples and groups of friends on a budget. We plan for them first. Every package comes in three tiers, Budget, Value and Comfort, and we tell you where a shared jeep or a homestay will do and where it will not.

A single planner shapes the route with you, applies for the permits, books every room and vehicle and stays on WhatsApp while you travel.

We write the way we plan. Our pages give distances in kilometres, drive times in hours, altitudes in metres and prices in rupees, and they say plainly when something changes year to year, like permits, road closures, park seasons and festival dates. Our fare board lists what shared jeeps and reserved cars cost on each route, so you can check our quotes against it.

Our photographs come from Wikimedia Commons under free licences, and each one names its photographer. We would rather show a credited picture of the real place than a styled one of somewhere else.

How a trip comes together

  1. You tell us the dates, who is travelling and roughly what you want to spend.
  2. We send a first route with a price in each tier.
  3. We refine it together and send a written quote with every cost on its own line.
  4. You book with a deposit; we confirm every service in writing.
  5. On the road, your planner is a message away.
