How we work

Three promises we keep

{% icon "rupee" %}Three tiers, every cost written down

Budget, Value or Comfort on every package. Your quote lists each room, car, permit and fee, so you can see where the money goes.

{% icon "jeep" %}Travel the way hill people do

Shared jeeps and homestays where they make sense, a registered private car where the permit or the road needs one.

{% icon "permit" %}Permits and roads handled

We apply for Nathu La, Tsomgo, North Sikkim and Bhutan permits, and plan a spare day for landslides and snow.
