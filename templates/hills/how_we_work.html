How we work

Five steps, no surprises

You pay nothing until the plan is right. Here is exactly what happens between your first message and your last drop at NJP.

{% include "hills/partials/trust.html" %}
Step01

You tell us the trip

Dates, how many of you, where you want to go and roughly what you want to spend. Use the form, a WhatsApp message or a call. Rough answers are fine.

Step02

A first route within one working day

Your planner sends a day-by-day route with drive times and a price in each tier: Budget, Value and Comfort. It is a draft to react to, not a sales pitch.

Step03

We refine it and itemise it

Swap a hotel for a homestay, drop a long drive, add a spare day for the passes. When it is right we send a written quote with every room, car, permit and fee on its own line.

Step04

You book with a deposit

A [25]% deposit confirms the booking; the balance is due [30] days before you travel. We confirm every room, vehicle and permit in writing. See payments and refunds and cancellations.

Step05

On the road, a message away

Your driver meets you at NJP, Bagdogra or the Siliguri stand. Your planner stays on WhatsApp while you travel and reroutes you if a landslide or snow closes a road.

What every trip includes

The basics, done properly

Three honest tiers

Budget, Value or Comfort, or a mix: homestays in the villages and one good hotel where it matters.

The right vehicle for each road

Shared jeeps where they make sense, a registered tourist vehicle where permits require one.

Permits handled

Tsomgo, Nathu La, North Sikkim, Dzongu and Bhutan permits applied for with the documents you send.

A spare day on mountain routes

Roads close for landslides and snow. We plan for it rather than hope it will not happen.

One planner throughout

The person who planned it is the person you message on the road.

An itemised quote

Every line priced, taxes shown, nothing hidden in a total.
