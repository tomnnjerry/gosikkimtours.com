# Content schema

Every page on gosikkimtours.com is rendered from the JSON files in this folder
(`hills/content.py` loads them). Validate a region with
`python tools/check_region.py <region-slug>` and the journal with
`python tools/check_journal.py`. Both must print `OK`.

```
content/
  _places.json            registry of every place slug, by region (do not invent places outside it)
  _slugs.json             generated by tools/build_slugs.py, used by the journal checker
  themes.json             the 14 travel styles
  images.json             generated by tools/fetch_images.py (Wikimedia Commons photos with credits)
  outlines.json           generated by tools/build_outlines.py (map outlines)
  <region>/region.json
  <region>/places/<place-slug>.json      one per place in _places.json
  <region>/journeys/<journey-slug>.json  3 per region
  <region>/stays.json                    list, 5–6 per region
  <region>/festivals.json                list, 2–3 per region
  <region>/routes.json                   list, 3–4 per region
  <region>/guides/<guide-slug>.json      2 per region
  journal/<post-slug>.json               blog posts (see JOURNAL_SCHEMA.md)
```

Region slugs: `east-sikkim`, `north-sikkim`, `west-sikkim` (covers West and South Sikkim),
`darjeeling`, `kalimpong`, `dooars`, `bhutan`.

## House style

- The business: **Go Sikkim Tours**, an affordable, honest trip planner for the Eastern Himalaya
  (Sikkim, Darjeeling, Kalimpong, the Dooars and Bhutan). Not luxury. Every package has three
  tiers: **Budget** (homestays, shared jeeps where possible), **Value** (good hotels, private car)
  and **Comfort** (the best rooms on the route).
- Plain British English, short sentences, concrete facts: heights in metres, distances in km,
  drive times in hours, money in INR written like `₹1,200` or `₹1,500–2,500`.
- Answer first. A summary is a direct answer a traveller could quote.
- Headings and titles never end with a full stop. Keep them under 70 characters.
- Never use these words or phrases: nestled, breathtaking, hidden gem, paradise, tapestry, embark,
  delve, unleash, vibrant, bustling, mesmerizing, stunning, magical, heaven on earth, feast for the eyes,
  something for everyone, whether you're, look no further, ultimate guide, in this blog, in conclusion,
  unforgettable, world-class, seamless, elevate, immerse, timeless, boasts, abode of, land of mystic,
  shangri-la, exotic, primitive. No exclamation marks anywhere.
- Prices, fares and permit rules are **indicative**. Where rules can change (permits, road closures,
  fees, park seasons) add "Check current status before you travel."
- Be accurate. Do not invent hotel names: use real, well-known properties or describe a homestay
  generically (e.g. "Lepcha homestays in Dzongu"). Do not invent statistics, quotes or sources.
- `wiki` is the exact title of an English Wikipedia article about the thing, or `""` if none exists.
  `image_query` is a short Wikimedia Commons search (e.g. "Tsomgo Lake Sikkim") used when `wiki` is empty
  or gives poor photos.
- Slugs: lowercase, hyphenated. Experience, journey, stay, festival, route and guide slugs are global
  across the site, so make them specific (e.g. `gangtok-mg-marg-evening-walk`, not `evening-walk`).

Allowed theme slugs (`themes`): shoestring, homestays, honeymoons, family-trips, snow-and-passes,
monasteries, tea-country, toy-train, treks-and-walks, wildlife-and-birds, festivals, food-trails,
photography, weekend-breaks.

Allowed experience kinds (`kind`): culture, spiritual, nature, wildlife, food, adventure, tea, snow,
craft, village.

`best_months` everywhere: 12 integers, January first; 2 = best, 1 = good, 0 = avoid/off season.

FAQ objects everywhere: `{"q": "...", "a": "..."}`. Answers 40–90 words, specific.

```json
{
  "slug": "mayfair-spa-resort-gangtok",
  "name": "Mayfair Spa Resort and Casino",
  "place": "gangtok",
  "kind": "Hotel | Homestay | Resort | Lodge | Heritage hotel | Farmstay | Camp",
  "budget": "Budget | Value | Comfort",
  "price_band": "₹2,000–3,500 per room per night",    // indicative
  "summary": "2 sentences.",
  "body": ["2 paragraphs"],
  "why": ["3–4 reasons we book it"],
  "best_for": ["Couples", "Families"],
  "website": "official URL or empty string",
  "wiki": "", "image_query": "...",
  "faqs": [3 faqs]
}
```
Use real, established properties for Value and Comfort. For Budget, prefer a real, well-known
homestay cluster or describe the kind of stay (e.g. "Lachung village homestays") without inventing
a business name.

## routes.json (list)

```json
{
  "slug": "siliguri-to-gangtok",
  "from": "siliguri", "to": "gangtok",                // place slugs from _places.json
  "distance_km": 114,
  "summary": "2 sentences: distance, time, best way.",
  "options": [                                         // 2–4; include a shared jeep row and a reserved car row where they exist
    {"mode": "Shared jeep", "time": "4–5 hours", "fare": "₹300–400 a seat", "text": "Where it leaves from, how it works."},
    {"mode": "Reserved car", "time": "4 hours", "fare": "₹3,500–4,500 per car", "text": "..."}
  ],
  "stops_on_way": ["2–4 stops"],
  "tip": "One practical tip.",
  "faqs": [4 faqs]
}
```
Name shared modes with the words "Shared jeep" or "Shared" and private modes with "Reserved car" or
"Private car" (the fare board looks for these words).
